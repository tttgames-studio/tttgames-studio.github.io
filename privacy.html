Club Empire: Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tristan Tirado ("we", "us") handles information in the Club Empire mobile game (the "Game"). We designed the Game to collect as little as possible.

1. What stays on your device

Your game progress (coins, club, stands, research, stars, memorabilia, cosmetics, settings), the club name you choose, and your age group (under 13 or 13 and older) are stored only on your device. We do not store your birth year, and we do not run accounts or servers that hold your game data.

2. Information collected by service providers

To show optional ads and process purchases, we use these providers. Each acts under its own privacy policy:

  • Advertising (Google AdMob): device advertising identifier (only with your permission where required), IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions, used to show rewarded ads you choose to watch.
  • Purchases (Apple App Store / Google Play, processed through RevenueCat): purchase receipts and an anonymous app user ID. We never see your payment card details.
  • Analytics: we do not use a third-party analytics service. Apple and Google may give us aggregated, anonymous statistics (such as installs, sessions and crashes) from players who agreed to share them with Apple or Google.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, the Game schedules reminders (for example, when scouts return) locally on your device. No information is sent to us.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.

3. Children

The Game asks for year of birth when it first opens, using a neutral age screen. For players under 13 we do not knowingly collect personal information: ads are contextual and non-personalized, and analytics identifiers are not sent. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it. This approach is intended to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

4. Your choices

  • Ads: rewarded ads are always optional. On iPhone you can allow or deny tracking when asked, and change it later in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in device settings.
  • Delete your data: Shop > Settings > Delete all my data erases everything the Game stores on your device. Uninstalling the Game does the same.
  • Provider data: to request access to or deletion of data held by our providers, email us and we will forward your request.

5. Your rights (EU, UK, California and elsewhere)

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Where required, we ask for consent before personalized ads. California residents may opt out of "sharing" for targeted advertising by declining tracking or resetting their advertising ID. Contact us to exercise any right; we will not discriminate against you for doing so.

6. Retention and security

Game data stays on your device until you delete it. Providers keep data according to their own policies. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method is completely secure.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the Game.
