Club Empire Privacy Policy

Effective November 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tristan Tirado-Rummo ("we", "us") handles information in the Club Empire mobile game (the "Game"). We designed the Game to collect as little as we need to run it, fund it with ads and in-app purchases, and improve it.

1. What stays on your device

Your game progress (coins, club, stands, research, stars, memorabilia, cosmetics, settings), the club name and badge you choose, your age group (under 13, 13, 14, 15, or 16 and older) with the date you answered, and your privacy choices are stored only on your device. We do not store your birth year, and we do not run accounts or servers that hold your game data.

2. Information collected by service providers

We use the providers below. Each acts under its own privacy policy.

  • Advertising (Google AdMob, policies.google.com/privacy): the Game shows optional rewarded ads you choose to watch and, for players 13 and older, occasional ads between matches. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone only if you tap Allow when asked), IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information, the ads you see and tap, and ad performance and crash information. It uses this to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. If you allow tracking (and, where the law requires it, give consent in Google's consent form), ads may be personalized, unless you switch personalized ads off in Settings > Ad privacy choices. Otherwise they are non-personalized. Buying "No Forced Ads" removes the ads between matches; rewarded ads stay available if you want their rewards.
  • Purchases (Apple App Store / Google Play, processed through RevenueCat, revenuecat.com/privacy): purchase receipts and history, an anonymous app user ID, IP address and device information, used to deliver and restore purchases. We never see your payment card details.
  • Apple Ads attribution (iPhone, players 13 and older): if you installed the Game after seeing one of our ads in the App Store, Apple gives the Game a token that RevenueCat uses to tell us which ad campaign led to the install. It does not include your advertising identifier and is not used to track you across other companies' apps.
  • Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Remote Config, policies.google.com/privacy), only for players the law allows us to measure: how the Game is used (for example screens opened, divisions reached, Legacy restarts, gems earned and spent, ads watched and purchases made, including App Store purchases Firebase records automatically), a few player facts (highest division, number of Legacies, whether you have made a purchase), a performance timing, a random app instance ID, device and app information, and an approximate location worked out from your IP address. We use it to understand and improve the Game and its offers. It does not use your device advertising identifier, is not linked to your name or contact details, and is not sold. Outside the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland, if you allow tracking and keep personalized ads on, Google may also receive signals that let it use this data for advertising. For the same players, the Game asks Firebase Remote Config for a few game settings (such as how often ads between matches show), using the same random ID. Players under 13 are never measured. In the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland it runs only if you say yes when the Game asks, and only if you are old enough to give that consent where you live (13 to 16, depending on the country). Elsewhere it runs for players 13 and older. You can switch it off or on at any time in Settings > Usage analytics.
  • Apple and Google may give us aggregated statistics (such as installs and crashes) from players who agreed to share them with developers.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, the Game schedules reminders (for example, when scouts return) locally on your device. No information is sent to us.

We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location, and we do not sell your personal information for money.

3. Children

The Game asks for month and year of birth when it first opens, using a neutral age screen, and keeps only the resulting age group. For players under 13 we do not knowingly collect personal information: ads are contextual and non-personalized, there are no ads between matches, there is no Apple Ads attribution, and analytics is off. In the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland, players under their country's age of digital consent (13 to 16) are treated the same way. This approach is intended to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

4. Your choices

  • Tracking: on iPhone you can allow or deny tracking when asked, and change it later in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in device settings.
  • Ad privacy: the Game's Settings > Ad privacy choices shows whether your ads are personalized and lets you switch personalized ads off or back on. In the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland it also lets you review or change your choice in Google's consent form.
  • Usage analytics: Settings > Usage analytics switches analytics off or on. When it is off, nothing more is sent.
  • Ads between matches: buying "No Forced Ads" removes them. Rewarded ads are always optional.
  • Delete your data: Settings (the gear at the top of the screen) > Delete all my data erases everything the Game stores on your device and gives this device a new analytics ID. Uninstalling the Game also erases it. Data our providers already received is not erased this way; email us and we will ask them to delete it.

5. Legal bases (EU/EEA and UK)

We rely on your consent for analytics and personalized ads, which you can withdraw at any time as described above; on performing our contract with you to deliver purchases; and on our legitimate interests to show non-personalized ads that fund the Game, measure which of our own ads bring players, keep the Game secure and prevent fraud.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA, the UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. California residents: when you allow tracking, sharing your advertising identifier with AdMob for personalized ads may count as "sharing" under California law. You can opt out by switching personalized ads off in Settings > Ad privacy choices, declining tracking, turning it off in your device settings, or resetting your advertising ID. Contact us to exercise any right; we will not discriminate against you for doing so.

7. International transfers

Our providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, they use safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Retention and security

Game data stays on your device until you delete it. Analytics event data is kept for up to 14 months. Other providers keep data according to their own policies. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method is completely secure.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the Game.
