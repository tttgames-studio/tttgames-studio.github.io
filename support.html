Club Empire: Support

Need help with the game, a purchase, or a data request? Email tttgames.support@gmail.com and include your device model and what happened.

Common questions

  • I bought something but didn't get it. Open Shop > Gems and tap Restore purchases. If it still isn't there, email us with the date of the purchase.
  • Can I get a refund? Purchases are processed by Apple or Google, so refunds are too: reportaproblem.apple.com for Apple, or your Google Play order history.
  • Is my progress saved online? No. Your club is saved only on this device, so deleting the game or its data erases it. "No Forced Ads" comes back with Restore purchases, but gems and other items can't be restored.
  • Can I turn off personalized ads? Yes, in Settings > Ad privacy choices.
  • How do I delete my data? Settings > Delete all my data. To delete data our providers hold, email us.
